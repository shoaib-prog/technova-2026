❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Who can participate in TechNova?

Students currently enrolled in colleges or universities can participate in TechNova 2026.


Is there an entry fee?

Registration is currently free for all participants.


Can I participate in multiple events?

Yes. Participants can register for multiple events as long as the event schedules do not overlap.


What should I bring to the event?

Participants should carry their valid college ID, registration confirmation and any required materials.


Will participants receive certificates?

Participation certificates will be provided according to the event guidelines.


How can I contact the organizers?

Visit our Contact Page for email, phone and location information.


Can I change my registration details?

Contact the registration team before the event for assistance with registration changes.
